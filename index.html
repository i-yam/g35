Our Philosophy

Counsel that changes the trajectory of a company deserves more than a slide deck.

The firm was founded on a narrow conviction: that generic frameworks applied to a specific company produce a document, not a decision. We start from the situation in front of us.

Every engagement is led by the people you meet first. There is no team behind the team, no juniors learning your business at your expense, and no deliverable whose purpose is to demonstrate effort.

We would rather be useful than impressive. In practice that means saying what we think, including when it is not what was hoped for, and leaving a client with something they can act on the same week.
